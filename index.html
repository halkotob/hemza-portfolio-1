We open on a grand dining hall where three people eat dinner together.

KING MERAS

Today, I met with my advisors about building a new wing to this castle - what do you all think we should name it?

A QUEEN, (50s), sitting to King Meras’s right, is kind, but knows how to get the best out of her husband.

QUEEN LILLIAN

I think naming it after someone in our lineage would be splendid, as long as that someone isn’t you.

A PRINCESS, (20s), laughs, sitting opposite Queen Lillian. She has her mother’s sense of humor, and her father’s boldness.

About

I’m a Muslim Syrian-American from Michigan. I studied computer science and film at the University of Michigan and graduated with a degree in each in 2024.

My scripts keep ending up in the same places: restaurant kitchens, wrestling rings, castles, basketball courts. I like characters who are hiding something and stories where the pieces click in the last act. Tracking down a production outage scratches the same itch.

I log every movie I watch on Letterboxd.
